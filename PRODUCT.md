# Blunt

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
android

## Stack
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Navigation Compose, ViewModels, StateFlow, coroutines, Coil. Android Studio project.

## Users
India-focused shoppers browsing multiple categories and claiming a single promotional Daily Drop. User confirmed the Blunt name and local demonstration scope.

## Product Purpose
A complete, polished shopping MVP: discover products, choose variants, save favorites, manage a cart and addresses, place a mock order, and track it.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Local fake catalogue of 18 products, local account registration/login, persisted profile/cart/wishlist/addresses/orders/onboarding. One successful claimant per Daily Drop, ten-minute reservation and payment only after a successful claim. No gambling, random selection, real payment processing, memberships, ads, seller tooling or server infrastructure. Mock offers, specifications, prices and reviews are illustrative.

## Brand Commitments
Premium modern commerce, spacious hierarchy, large product imagery, neutral surfaces with one strong accent, restrained motion. Native Android affordances remain familiar. No excessive gradients, glows, or rounded panels.

## Evidence on Hand
Full user specification in the supplied Pasted text.txt attachment. No commercial catalogue, logo, legal policy, or backend was supplied; demonstration content must be labeled as such.

## Product Principles
Complete tasks end to end. State tells the truth. Every important control works. Repository contracts allow replacing local data with an API. Failure states explain a recovery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable scalable text, 48dp touch targets, content descriptions, system Back, keyboard-safe forms and reduced animation support.
